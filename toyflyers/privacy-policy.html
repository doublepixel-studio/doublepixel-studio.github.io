This policy explains what information the mobile game Toy Flyers (the "Game", com.doublepixel.toyflyers), an online racing game of small flying toys, uses, why, and what you can do about it. The Game is made and published by Bar Sagiv, an independent developer in Israel, under the studio name DoublePixel ("we", "us"); shown on Google Play as ArcLogic Studio and on the App Store as Bar Sagiv. All three names are the same publisher. Bar Sagiv is responsible for this information (its "controller" under data-protection law). Questions: barsagivv@gmail.com.

In short
  • You play as a guest with an anonymous account. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and keeps your progress.
  • Other players see only your public profile, with the nickname you choose. There is no chat: only six preset emoji faces.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. Your advertising ID is used only with your consent.
  • Payments go through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Settings → Account → Delete account deletes everything at once.

1. Your game account

The Game signs you in as a guest with Firebase Authentication (Google), which creates an anonymous account with a random account id. No email, password or real name is needed.

Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple (optional). You can connect your account to Google or Apple to keep your progress. We then receive your account id at that provider and the email address and name that the provider shares with us. We use them only to sign you in and keep your progress. The Game never shows them to other players: it shows only a nickname that you choose.

2. What our server stores

Our server is Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Functions, in Google Cloud's europe-west1 region (Belgium). Linked to your account id, it stores:

  • Cloud save: your game progress.
  • Public profile: your nickname, your flyer, your trophies, your arena, a country code taken from your device's region setting, and when you were last seen. Other players can see it.
  • Leaderboards and the weekly league: your scores and places.
  • Friends: your friends list and your friend code.
  • Online presence: whether you are online, while the Game is open.
  • Races: your presses during a live race, which the other racers receive, and race replays that are shared with other players.

There is no chat and no free text between players: during a race you can send only one of six preset emoji faces.

3. Notifications

The Game uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send push notifications about friend requests and race invites. For this it keeps a device token, a random id for your phone's notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

4. Advertising

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and ads shown between races. Players who have bought anything in the Game never see ads they did not choose.

  • The Game asks for your consent with Google's consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform). Your advertising ID (on Android the advertising ID, on iPhone the IDFA) is used only with your consent.
  • On iPhone, the IDFA is used only if you also allow tracking when the Game asks (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, the Game works the same.
  • AdMob may also collect your IP address and the approximate location it shows (not GPS), information about your device and the app, and which ads were shown and tapped, with diagnostics. Google uses this to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are optional and go through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Our server checks the store's transaction id for each purchase and keeps it, so that the same purchase cannot be used twice.

6. Analytics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to understand how the Game is played: app usage events, information about your device, and an app instance id.

7. Why we use it

  • To run the Game and its online features (account, cloud save, profile, leaderboards, league, friends, races, notifications): to provide the Game you asked for (our agreement with you, the Terms of Use).
  • To keep purchases fair and the service secure: our legitimate interest.
  • To show ads that keep the Game free: your consent for the advertising ID; otherwise our legitimate interest.
  • To improve the Game with analytics: our legitimate interest, or your consent where the law requires it.

8. Who receives it

We share information only with the services that run the Game for us: Google (Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Analytics, AdMob, Google Play, Sign in with Google) and Apple (App Store, App Tracking Transparency, Sign in with Apple). Other players see your public profile, your scores and places, your race presses and the replays that are shared. We may also disclose information when the law requires it. We do not sell your data. Their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Apple Privacy Policy

9. Where it is stored

Our server's data is stored in Google Cloud's europe-west1 region (Belgium). Google and Apple may also process data in other countries under their own data-protection terms. We run the Game from Israel.

10. How long we keep it

We keep your data until your account is deleted. If you play as a guest, never sign in, and uninstall the Game, your guest account cannot be opened again and its data stays on our server, unreadable to anyone through the Game, until you ask us to delete it (section 11).

11. Deleting your data

In the Game: Settings → Account → Delete account deletes your account and all its data at once. Or email barsagivv@gmail.com with the friend code shown in your profile, and we delete it within 30 days. Details: Delete your account and data.

12. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, and, where the law gives you these rights (for example under the GDPR), to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority). Email barsagivv@gmail.com with your friend code; we reply within 30 days.

13. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, email us and we will delete the data.

14. Security

Data sent between the Game and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

16. Contact

DoublePixel (Bar Sagiv; shown on Google Play as ArcLogic Studio and on the App Store as Bar Sagiv) · barsagivv@gmail.com
