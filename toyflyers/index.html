Toy Flyers

by DoublePixel · Last updated 9 November 2026

An online racing game of small flying toys. Race other players, climb the leaderboards and the weekly league, and race your friends.

עברית ↓

How it plays

  • Online races against other players, with leaderboards and a weekly league.
  • Add friends with a friend code and invite them to a race.
  • Players appear with a nickname they choose. There is no chat: only six preset emoji faces.
  • Play as a guest, or sign in with Google or Apple to keep your progress.

Free to play

Toy Flyers is free and supported by ads: rewarded ads you choose to watch, and ads between races. In-app purchases are optional, and players who have bought anything never see ads they did not choose. For Android phones (Google Play) and iPhone (App Store).

Support and contact

Toy Flyers is made by Bar Sagiv under the studio name DoublePixel. On Google Play the developer is shown as ArcLogic Studio, and on the App Store the seller is Bar Sagiv.
