Privacy Policy

Gravity Bop · DoublePixel Studio · Last updated 28 October 2026
עברית ↓
In short: this version of Gravity Bop works completely offline. It does not send your information to us or to anyone else. It has no account, no ads, no purchases and no analytics.

Gravity Bop (the "Game") is made and published by Bar Sagiv, an independent developer in Israel, under the studio name DoublePixel Studio ("we", "us"). On Google Play the developer is shown as DoublePixel Studio. Bar Sagiv is responsible for this information.

1. On your phone

The game saves your progress on your device: levels cleared, Gravity Shards, coins, the avatars and effects you unlocked, missions and achievements, and your settings. This information stays in the app's private storage. It is not backed up to the cloud by the app, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game. Settings → Reset progress erases your level progress at any time.

2. No account and no server

You do not sign in and you do not create an account. This version does not connect to the internet at all (it does not even request the Android network permission), so nothing you do in the game reaches our servers.

3. Advertising

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. It does not use the advertising ID.

4. Purchases

This version has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent inside the game.

5. Analytics and crash reports

The game itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools. If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics. That data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Testing programme

If you join the Gravity Bop test through Google Play, Google manages your enrolment under its own policies; we see only what Google Play shows developers about testers. If you send us feedback by email or through a testing community, we receive what you choose to send (for example your email address, a description, screenshots) and use it only to answer you and to fix the game. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

7. Children

Gravity Bop is a general-audience game. Because this version collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

8. Your rights

We hold no personal information from the game. For feedback you sent us by email, you can ask for access, correction or deletion using the contact below.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online features (such as leaderboards), ads or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.

10. Contact

DoublePixel Studio (Bar Sagiv) — barsagivv@gmail.com
