This policy explains what information the mobile game Gravity Bop (the "Game", com.doublepixel.gravityswitch), a one-touch gravity runner, uses, why, and what you can do about it. The Game is made and published by Bar Sagiv, an independent developer in Israel, under the studio name DoublePixel ("we", "us"); shown on the App Store as Bar Sagiv and on Google Play as DoublePixel Studio. These names are the same publisher. Bar Sagiv is responsible for this information (its "controller" under data-protection law). Questions: doublepixel26@gmail.com.

Which version this applies to

The Android test version currently distributed through Google Play works offline: it has no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no online features, and it sends nothing to us. For it, only sections 1, 14, 15, 16 and 18 and the crash-statistics paragraph of section 7 apply. The other sections apply to versions that have those features: today, the iPhone version on the App Store.

In short
  • Your progress, coins and items are saved on your phone.
  • Online features use an anonymous account, created automatically. No name, email, phone number or sign-in.
  • Leaderboards show a name the Game generates. There is no chat and no text typed by players.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, with Google's consent message where the law requires it. The Game never asks to track you, so on iPhone it does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your card.
  • Analytics are anonymous (Aptabase) and not linked to your account.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Settings → DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT deletes your online data.

1. On your phone

The Game saves your progress on your device: levels cleared, Gravity Shards, coins, the items you unlocked or bought, missions and achievements, and your settings. This save stays in the app's private storage. The Game does not back it up, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game. Settings → RESET PROGRESS erases your level progress at any time.

2. Your online account

When you use online features, the Game automatically creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication (Google Firebase). It has no name, email, phone number or password, and you never sign in. The account has a random account id, and a random id created for each installation tells your installs apart. Our server runs on Google Cloud and Firebase in the me-west1 region (Tel Aviv, Israel).

3. What our server stores

Linked to your anonymous account, our server stores:

  • Leaderboards: your scores and distances on the Endless boards (all-time and weekly) and the Daily Challenge boards, with your generated display name.
  • Daily Gift: your daily streak and recent claims. The server decides the day and the reward.
  • Campaign progress check: which levels you cleared and when, used as a plausibility check.
  • Accepted run records: kept for 30 days so that the same run cannot be submitted twice.
  • Rate-limit counters: how often requests were made, to protect the service from abuse.
  • Push devices: if you turned on server reminders (section 8).
  • Purchase records: see section 5.

4. Leaderboards and other players

Other players see your generated display name with your scores and distances on the leaderboards. You cannot type a name, and the Game has no chat and no text written by players.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are optional and go through Apple In-App Purchase. We never see your card or payment details. Before granting a purchase, our server asks Apple's App Store Server API to confirm the transaction. It keeps a purchase record (a hash of the transaction, the product, its status and dates) to handle refunds and prevent fraud.

6. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): short videos you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ads at some breaks between levels and between runs. Full-screen ads never appear during play or in the first five levels, and there is always a minimum time between two of them; we may adjust how often they appear. The NO ADS purchase removes the full-screen ads; the optional reward videos stay available.

  • To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device and usage data, such as your approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • The Game never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, so on iPhone it does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Before ads appear, players in regions that require it (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) are asked for consent through Google's consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform). Players in US states with privacy laws see Google's US-state privacy message, which lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for ads. Where these rules apply, you can change your choice at any time in Settings → AD PRIVACY. If you do not consent, ads may still be shown without personalization.
  • The Game never asks for permission to use your location, so neither the Game nor Google's ad SDK can read your phone's precise (GPS) location. The only location involved is the approximate one Google may estimate from your IP address, described above.

7. Analytics and crash reports

The Game uses Aptabase, privacy-first analytics, to understand how the Game is played. It receives anonymous gameplay events (for example a level started or completed, a screen used, an error as a short code), the app version, your phone's operating system and its version, the language setting, and a random session id that changes after an hour without play. It receives no device or advertising identifiers and no personal data, and it is not linked to your online account. Aptabase's server works out your country and region from your IP address and uses the IP address, with a key that changes every day, to count daily players anonymously; it does not store your IP address. Aptabase keeps this analytics data for up to five years.

If you allowed your phone to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google Play may also give us anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics under their own policies.

8. Notifications

The Game sends reminders (for example, that your Daily Gift is ready) only after you say yes in the Game and in your phone's system prompt. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Where server reminders are enabled, a push token (Apple Push Notification service, through Firebase Cloud Messaging) is stored linked to your anonymous account; you get at most one reminder a day, and none in quiet hours. You can turn reminders off in the Game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

9. Why we use it

  • To run the Game and the online features you use (account, leaderboards, Daily Challenge, Daily Gift, reminders): to provide the Game you asked for.
  • To keep leaderboards and purchases fair and the service secure (plausibility checks, duplicate prevention, rate limits, purchase records): our legitimate interest.
  • To show ads that keep the Game free: your consent where the law requires it; otherwise our legitimate interest.
  • To improve the Game with anonymous analytics: our legitimate interest.

10. Who receives it

We share information only with the services that run the Game for us: Google (Firebase Authentication, Google Cloud and Firebase servers, Firebase Cloud Messaging, AdMob and its consent tool, Google Play), Apple (App Store, In-App Purchase, the App Store Server API, Apple Push Notification service) and Aptabase (analytics). Other players see your generated display name and your leaderboard results. We may also disclose information when the law requires it. We do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Aptabase

11. Where it is stored

Our server's data is stored in Google Cloud's me-west1 region (Tel Aviv, Israel). Google, Apple and Aptabase may process data in other countries under their own data-protection terms. We run the Game from Israel.

12. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the Game.
  • Online data: as long as your online account exists, until you delete it (section 13). Accepted run records are deleted after 30 days.
  • Purchase records: kept after your account is deleted, without any link to you, for refunds and to prevent the same purchase from being used twice.
  • If you uninstall the Game without deleting your online account first, the Game can no longer reach that account and its data stays on our server. You can ask us by email, but because the account has no name or email, we may not be able to find it. Please delete your online account in the Game before uninstalling.

13. Deleting your data

In the Game: Settings → DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT deletes your server profile, leaderboard entries, run records, Daily Gift streak, Campaign progress record, rate-limit counters, push devices and the anonymous account. Purchase records stay without any link to you, and a later Restore still works. Your local save stays on your phone until you uninstall the Game. Details: Delete your online account.

14. Testing programme

If you join a Gravity Bop test through Google Play or Apple TestFlight, Google or Apple manages your enrolment under its own policies; we see only what they show developers about testers. If you send us feedback by email or through a testing community, we receive what you choose to send (for example your email address, a description, screenshots) and use it only to answer you and to fix the Game. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

15. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, and, where the law gives you these rights (for example under the GDPR), to restrict or object to its use, to receive a copy, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority). Email doublepixel26@gmail.com; we reply within one month, the time data-protection laws such as the GDPR allow. Because your online account is anonymous, the in-game options are the surest way to act on your own online data.

16. Children

Gravity Bop is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children, it is not in the App Store's Kids category, and its ads are not set up as ads for children. The Game never asks for a player's age, name, email or any contact details, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age set by the law of their country). The Game's age rating describes its content, not who it is made for. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child used the Game's online features, use Settings → DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT on that phone, or email us and we will help.

17. Security

Data sent between the Game and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

18. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Contact: DoublePixel (Bar Sagiv; shown on the App Store as Bar Sagiv and on Google Play as DoublePixel Studio) · doublepixel26@gmail.com
