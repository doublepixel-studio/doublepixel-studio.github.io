Gravity Bop

by DoublePixel · Last updated 10 November 2026

A one-touch gravity runner. Hold to send gravity up, release to bring it back down, and guide your runner through six Worlds without touching a hazard.

עברית ↓

How it plays

  • One touch: hold anywhere and gravity points up; let go and it points down. No buttons, no joystick.
  • Six Worlds of 40 levels each, with three Gravity Shards to collect in every level.
  • Endless mode: run as far as you can and chase your best distance.
  • Daily Challenge: the same run for every player each day.
  • Leaderboards for Endless (all-time and weekly) and for the Daily Challenge. Players appear with a name the Game generates; there is no chat and no text typed by players.
  • Earn coins as you play and unlock avatars, skins, trails and effects in the Shop.

Versions

  • iPhone (App Store): free to play, with the online features above, optional ads (videos you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ads between levels and runs, never in the first five levels) and optional in-app purchases. The NO ADS purchase removes the full-screen ads; the optional reward videos stay available.
  • Android (Google Play test version): the test version currently distributed works offline. It has no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no online features.

Help

  • Restore purchases: open the Shop and tap RESTORE, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Permanent purchases such as NO ADS come back; coin packs are used when bought and cannot be restored.
  • Lost progress after reinstalling or on a new phone? Your progress, coins and items are saved on your device only. Gravity Bop has no cloud save, so it cannot bring them back after the Game is removed or on another phone (purchases can be restored as above).
  • Reminders on or off: in the Game's Settings, or in your phone's settings.
  • Ad choices: where your region's rules require it, Settings → AD PRIVACY lets you change your consent choice.
  • Delete your online account: Settings → DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT, or see the page below.
  • Something not working? Email us your phone model, its system version and what happened. A screenshot helps.
