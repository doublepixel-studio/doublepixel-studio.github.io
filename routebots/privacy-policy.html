In short: RouteBots keeps your progress on your device. You never sign in. Test versions show Google test ads; where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first. The game has no analytics, no purchases and no account.

RouteBots (the "Game") is made and published by Bar Sagiv, an independent developer in Israel, under the studio name DoublePixel Studio ("we", "us"). On Google Play the developer is shown as DoublePixel Studio. Bar Sagiv is responsible for this information.

1. On your phone

The game saves your progress, discoveries, collection and settings in the app's private storage. It has no cloud save and no account: removing the game removes your progress.

2. Advertising in test versions

Test versions of RouteBots (Google Play testing, TestFlight) show test ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), never real advertisers: an optional video you choose to watch for a hint or a bonus. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (on Android the advertising ID; on iOS only if you allowed tracking, which RouteBots never asks for), your approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics. In regions that require it (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice in Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. No account, no server, no analytics

RouteBots does not send your progress or gameplay to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools and no in-app purchases.

4. Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules local notifications on your phone. Nothing leaves the device.

5. Testing programme

If you join a RouteBots test through Google Play or TestFlight, Google or Apple manages your enrolment under its own policies; we see only what the store shows developers about testers. If you send us feedback by email or through a testing community, we receive what you choose to send and use it only to answer you and to fix the game. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

6. Children

RouteBots is a general-audience game, not directed at children. It never asks for a name, email, contacts or location.

7. Changes to this policy

Before a version adds real ads, analytics, accounts, purchases or online features, we will update this policy and change the date above.

8. Contact

DoublePixel Studio (Bar Sagiv) — barsagivv@gmail.com
